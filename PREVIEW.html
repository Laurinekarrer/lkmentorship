A NEW ERA OF CONNECTION

Finding your people is part of finding yourself.

Making a life in a new city can be exciting and a little lonely. The Becoming Club was created for women who want more than small talk: beautiful places to discover, genuine people to meet, and space to reconnect with the person they are becoming.

A new city. A new season. A little more of yourself.
