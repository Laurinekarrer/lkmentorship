BECOMING SESSIONS 01 — OPENING SPEECH (around 60–90 seconds)

Hi girls, thank you so much for joining this first Becoming Session.

If we haven't met yet, I'm Laurine. I'm originally from France, and I've spent years travelling and living abroad. I'm also a certified sophrologist and former beauty therapist, and I've spent a lot of my professional life working with women.

I created The Becoming Club because I wanted a space where we could connect without networking pressure or feeling we need to fit a spiritual label. The Becoming Sessions are its more reflective side: a small, guided circle where we can make room for ourselves too.

Today's theme is “Who Am I Becoming?” We're going to explore identity beyond roles and expectations. We'll use a little grounding, journaling and conversation, inspired by my Identity Alignment Workbook.

You can share as much or as little as you want. You are welcome to pass on any question or exercise. We respect each other's privacy and listen without trying to fix one another.

You don't need to walk away with a dramatic breakthrough. If you leave with one clearer thought or one kind next step, that's enough.

Let's begin with a gentle check-in.
