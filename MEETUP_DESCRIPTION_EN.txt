TITLE: Who Am I Becoming? | Becoming Session 01 · The Becoming Club

A little clarity. A little more of yourself.

Have you ever felt like you're changing, but you're not quite sure who you're becoming?

Moving abroad, starting over, exploring relationships, work and personal expectations can leave us wondering what feels genuinely ours.

Becoming Sessions is an intimate guided circle for women who want to pause, reconnect and get a little clearer — without pressure to have everything figured out.

OUR FIRST THEME — WHO AM I BECOMING?

Identity beyond roles and expectations.

Expect a warm welcome, gentle body awareness and sophrology exercises, guided journaling, honest conversations (sharing is always optional), and a personal intention to take into your week.

FIVE THEMES. ONE UNFOLDING JOURNEY.
01 — Who Am I Becoming? / Identity beyond roles.
02 — Finding Your Place / Belonging and feeling at home.
03 — The Patterns We Carry / Choices and reactions we repeat.
04 — From Confusion to Clarity / What feels in and out of sync.
05 — Move From Self-Trust / One honest next step.

Each gathering stands alone, while joining all five gives you more space to revisit your reflections and develop them over time.

HOSTED BY LAURINE KARRER
Certified sophrologist, former beauty therapist, solo traveller and founder of Laurine Karrer Studio. These sessions are inspired by Laurine's Identity Alignment Workbook.
