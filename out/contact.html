Useful answers

FAQ

How do I know which session is right for me?+

You do not need to decide alone. Share what you are currently experiencing and what you would like support with, and we will identify the most relevant format together.

Are sessions available in person and online?+

Yes. In-person sessions take place in Amsterdam and selected partner spaces. Sophrology Sessions are also available online.

How do I book and pay?+

You can begin through WhatsApp or the contact form. Your time, location, booking link and payment details will be confirmed before your appointment.

How should I prepare for my session?+

Each service page includes preparation guidance adapted to that experience. In general, choose comfortable clothing, bring water and arrive with enough time to settle.

What should I share before a body treatment or class?+

Please mention any pregnancy, injury, allergy, medical condition or contraindication before your appointment so the session can be safely adapted to you.

Which languages are available?+

Sessions, classes and conversations are available in English and French.
