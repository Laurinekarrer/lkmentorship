Body & Intuitive Care

Frequently asked questions.

What makes the Body Ritual Massage different from a classic massage?+

This is not only a sequence of massage techniques. It is a slow, experiential full-body ritual shaped by what your body communicates in the moment. Intuitive touch, deep relaxation and focused abdominal work create space to notice and release physical and emotional tension without having to explain everything in words. It can be a gentle first doorway into my practice if you need care and reconnection before choosing a spoken Sophrology Session.

Why does the ritual include abdominal work?+

The abdomen is closely connected to breathing, the nervous system, digestion and the way stress or emotion can be felt physically. With your consent, gentle and attentive abdominal touch can support relaxation, awareness and a sense of inner space. Pressure and positioning are always adapted, and this part can be changed or omitted at any time.

What is the Face Ritual Massage?+

The Face Ritual Massage is a 90-minute upper-body and facial ritual combining lymphatic drainage, sculpting massage, skincare and conscious relaxation. The detox phase supports circulation and the elimination of retained fluids, while the restorative phase helps soften visible signs of stress and ageing and supports the skin’s natural collagen and elastin functions. It is designed to leave the face looking smoother, fuller and more luminous while helping the mind slow down.

Who is the Face Ritual Massage for?+

It is especially suited to people who want a deeply relaxing massage experience without undressing for a full-body treatment. Because the ritual is centred on the face, scalp, neck, shoulders and upper body, it can be supportive when you feel mentally overwhelmed, caught in overthinking, tense around the jaw and shoulders, or prone to stress-related headaches. Please seek medical advice for persistent or unexplained headaches.

Is Guidance a psychic or predictive card reading?+

Not exactly. I use situational cards through an intuitive coaching method I have developed for personal, relational and professional questions. The intention is not to predict a fixed future, remove your responsibility or tell you what decision to make. I take a wider, “hawk’s-eye” view of the situation to reflect what may be happening now, what it is challenging or transforming in your life, and what perspective may help you move forward with greater clarity and self-trust.

Are there contraindications to body or facial treatments?+

Please share any pregnancy, recent surgery, injury, skin condition, allergy, infection, fever, circulatory concern, acute inflammation or relevant medical treatment before booking. The session may be adapted, postponed or require medical approval. These treatments support wellbeing and relaxation and do not replace medical care.

What to expect

A space adapted to you.

We begin by arriving, noticing what is present and agreeing on your intention. The session unfolds with care and closes with time for integration.

Come in comfortable, loose clothing and simple undergarments. Bring a water bottle and earplugs if you wish. If you have one or more meaningful personal objects—such as jewellery or crystals—that you would like to purify during the session, you are welcome to bring them with you.

Please share any medical condition, pregnancy, injury, allergy or contraindication before booking a body or facial treatment so the experience can be safely adapted to you.
