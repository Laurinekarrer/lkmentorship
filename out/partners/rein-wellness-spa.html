About the space

Relax. Refresh. Recharge.

Rein Wellness Spa is an oasis of calm in Amsterdam-West. Alongside its private spa, magnesium floating and Japanese head spa, the space offers a dedicated baby spa where parents and babies can slow down, connect and experience floating and nurturing touch together.

Why this partnership

Care begins with listening.

Rein Wellness Spa offers a natural setting for bringing body awareness into the relationship between parent and baby. Floating creates space for free movement, while conscious touch encourages parents to slow down and respond to their baby’s individual rhythm.

This partnership extends my work around presence and body listening into the earliest stages of connection, while respecting comfort, consent cues and each baby’s needs.
