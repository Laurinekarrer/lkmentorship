About the space

The cold heart of the city.

Located on Cruquiuseiland in Amsterdam-Oost, LUCA is a warm and stylish wellness space built around health, relaxation and community. The space brings together sauna and ice-bath rituals, massage, art, a bar and inspiring places to work and connect.
