# Session 01 — Who Am I Becoming? · 90 minutes

*Design intention:* a light, structured self-reflection circle — not a therapy session, tarot reading or lecture.

| Time | Part | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Arrive and settle | Welcome, soft introductions, drinks, agenda and agreements. |
| 10–20 min | Guided grounding | A comfortable posture, awareness of feet and breath. Participation is optional; eyes may remain open. No breath-holding. |
| 20–35 min | Identity check-in | Write three roles you carry: e.g. friend, professional, expat. Which currently feels most demanding? |
| 35–55 min | Journaling | "Where am I changing?", "What part of me wants more room?", "What expectations am I ready to question?" |
| 55–75 min | Shared reflection | Invite voluntary sharing: "Which answer surprised you?" Refrain from unsolicited advice, diagnoses or attempting to fix others. |
| 75–85 min | A small next step | Each woman picks one gentle intention or action within the next seven days. |
| 85–90 min | Closing | Thank everyone, mention the five-theme sequence and optional Identity Alignment Workbook as further reading. No sales pressure. |

## Session guidelines
- Confidentiality requested, though never promise absolute confidentiality among participants.
- Sharing is optional; no mental health or medical information is required.
- Invite equal airtime; don't allow one participant to dominate.
- Respect personal boundaries and the right to step out.
- Ask permission before photos; don't assume consent.

## The series — proposed thematic arc
01 — Who Am I Becoming? (identity)
02 — Finding Your Place (belonging)
03 — The Patterns We Carry (habits and reactions)
04 — From Confusion to Clarity (priorities)
05 — Move From Self-Trust (next step)

**Suggested cadence:** Mondays 14:00–15:30 for this pilot, but announce dates 02–05 only after checking venue availability and interest. Avoid publishing unconfirmed dates as confirmed events.

**Optional workbook mention at closing:**
"Today's reflection was inspired by my Identity Alignment Workbook. If you'd like a more personal space to continue these questions between gatherings, I'll share the optional link afterwards. There's absolutely no pressure to purchase anything."
