COCO 02 | Coffee & Connection at Café Belcampo ☕🤍

Come for the coffee. Stay for the connection.

After our first CoCo, we're opening another little circle for women who want to meet, share stories and discover cosy places in Amsterdam — without networking, labels or any pressure to perform.

Whether you're new to the city or have been here for years, you're welcome to come on your own. We'll start with a light conversation prompt, then let the afternoon flow naturally. Dating, work, travel, friendship, everyday life... real conversations about whatever matters to us.

WHAT TO EXPECT
• A small, welcoming circle — 6 women + Laurine
• 90 minutes of casual conversation
• A lovely café to discover in Amsterdam Oud-West
• Space to laugh, share and meet women you might want to see again
