Read our guidelines ↓

HOW WE GATHER

Belong without performing.

A space where we aim to feel safe enough to be ourselves, while respecting each other's boundaries.

  • Come as you are. No performance needed.
    You don't need to impress, prove anything or be a certain version of yourself. Just show up as you are, whatever your mood or energy.
  • Slow down & reconnect.
    Take a moment to settle in, notice how you're feeling and reconnect with yourself. This is a space to pause, not another place to perform.
  • Different opinions, open minds.
    You're welcome to have your own views, even when they differ from others. Stay curious, question perspectives and make room for differences without judging the person behind them.
  • Make room for every voice.
    Share your thoughts, listen when others speak and respect everyone's rhythm. You can speak, disagree or simply listen. Nothing personal needs to be shared.
  • Connection over networking.
    No sales pitches, no self-promotion and no pressure to exchange contacts. Respect each other's privacy: no sharing personal stories or photos without permission. We're here for genuine conversations, not opportunities.

No perfect answers. No expectations. Just a space to slow down, connect and be human.
